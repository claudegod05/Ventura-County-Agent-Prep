# Ventura County Multifamily Market: Question Bank

## About This File

This is a study question bank for mastering the Ventura County multifamily market over a two-month period. It is intended as source content for a study website.

- 220 questions across 17 sections.
- Questions are numbered continuously across sections; treat the number as a stable ID (e.g., Q132).
- Parenthetical notes inside a question are research prompts, not answers.
- Answers are not included yet. Each question should eventually support an answer, sources, and a last-verified date, since laws, rates, and market figures change.

---
